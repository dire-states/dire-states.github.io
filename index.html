What is the Filecoin chain doing? message-mix counts every message on mainnet and calibnet by the receiving actor type and method, such as miner.SubmitWindowedPoSt or evm.InvokeContract, in hourly buckets, and shows the result as a time series you can browse.

When is locked FIL released? fil-release reads every miner's vesting block rewards and the initial pledge of each live sector on mainnet and calibnet, and shows when they unlock: per miner, per day, and for the whole network.

public-mpool

What's waiting to get on chain? public-mpool watches the Filecoin mainnet mempool through public RPC nodes (Glif, Zondax, dRPC, Ankr) while the page is open. It lists pending messages with their fees, age and full JSON, charts mempool size and premiums, measures how long messages wait before inclusion, and shows how each node's mempool differs.

Live only: nothing is stored, so it shows what happened since you opened it.
